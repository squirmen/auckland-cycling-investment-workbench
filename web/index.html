    Map notes

    About this map

    What it is

    The workbench compares candidate cycling corridors across demand scenarios, planning lenses, budgets, network effects, and evidence quality.

    Use with care

    This is an indicative research screen, not an investment business case. Results depend on the stated inputs and assumptions; local design, safety, delivery, cost, and consultation work is still required.

    Data and map credits

    Light and Streets request OpenFreeMap vector tiles and use OpenStreetMap data. Analysis is the tile-free view used for reproducible captures.
