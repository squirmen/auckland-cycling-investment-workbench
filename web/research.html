Planning use and evidence

Use this experiment to compare connected packages, inspect full routes and export proposals for GIS review. The main explorer screens Auckland-wide scenarios; this page tests new routing on a bounded local network.

For an investment decision: validate OD sampling and observed routes, inspect crossings and design feasibility, confirm programmed projects and costs, and test who gains access. Ridership, safety benefits and benefit–cost ratios need separate calibration. No state-of-the-art performance or novel-algorithm claim has yet been established.
