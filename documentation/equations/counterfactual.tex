For candidate $C\subseteq\Edges$ and declared treatment cost $c_e^{C}$,
\begin{equation}
c_e^{(1,C)}=
\begin{cases}
c_e^{C}, & e\in C,\\
c_e, & e\notin C.
\end{cases}
\label{eq:treated-cost}
\end{equation}
The treated route and its generalized cost are
\begin{align}
P_i^{(1,C)}
 &=\arg\min_{P\in\Paths_i}\sum_{e\in P}c_e^{(1,C)},
\label{eq:treated-path}\\
C_i^{0}
 &=\sum_{e\in P_i^{0}}c_e,
\qquad
C_i^{1,C}=\sum_{e\in P_i^{(1,C)}}c_e^{(1,C)}.
\label{eq:path-costs}
\end{align}
Let $B_i$ be the baseline cycling count used for the comparison,
$q_i=B_i/E_i$, and $\epsilon$ the declared probability floor. Define
$\widetilde p_i^0=\min\{1-\epsilon,\max(\epsilon,q_i)\}$. The bounded
odds-elasticity response with $\eta\ge0$ is
\begin{align}
\logit\!\left(p_i^{1,C}\right)
 &= \logit\!\left(\widetilde p_i^{0}\right)
 + \eta\max\!\left\{0,\log\!\left(\frac{C_i^{0}}{C_i^{1,C}}\right)\right\},
\label{eq:odds-elasticity}\\
\Delta A_i^C
 &= \min\!\left\{E_i-B_i,\max(0,E_ip_i^{1,C}-B_i)\right\}
 I\!\left(C\cap P_i^{(1,C)}\ne\varnothing\right).
\label{eq:incremental-trips}
\end{align}
The path-use indicator prevents a corridor from receiving benefit when its
network change does not appear on the treated path. Subtracting $B_i$, rather
than $E_i\widetilde p_i^0$, ensures the numerical floor does not erase a
possible increment for a disclosure-controlled zero. The response elasticity
and floor are declared sensitivities unless locally estimated.
