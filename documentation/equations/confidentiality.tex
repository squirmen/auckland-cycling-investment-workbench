For count field $X\in\{E,O\}$ in OD relation $i$, let $r_{Xi}$ be an
unsuppressed numeric 2023 Census release and $s_{Xi}$ indicate that the
publisher supplied an explicit suppression marker in a sensitive table. Here
$E$ is the total stated journey-to-work count in the OD cell and $O$ is its
bicycle subset. Stats NZ's fixed-random-rounding rule gives
$r_{Xi}\in3\mathbb{Z}_{\ge0}$ and
\begin{equation}
  |r_{Xi}-X_i^{\mathrm{true}}|\le2
  \quad\text{when }s_{Xi}=0.
\label{eq:frr3-bound}
\end{equation}
Define the conservative field interval
\begin{equation}
[X_i^L,X_i^U]=
\begin{cases}
[0,5], & s_{Xi}=1,\\
[\max(0,r_{Xi}-2),r_{Xi}+2], & s_{Xi}=0.
\end{cases}
\label{eq:censored-count-interval}
\end{equation}
Thus an unsuppressed numeric zero has interval $[0,2]$; it is not
interchangeable with a suppression marker. Eligible and bicycle counts are not
selected independently. Their joint feasible set and declared selector are
\begin{equation}
\mathcal J_i=
\left\{(E,O):E_i^L\le E\le E_i^U,\;
O_i^L\le O\le O_i^U,\;0\le O\le E\right\},
\qquad
(E_i^*,O_i^*)=g_{\boldsymbol\kappa}(\mathcal J_i).
\label{eq:censored-count-point}
\end{equation}
An empty $\mathcal J_i$ is an import error. Uncertainty draws also have support
only on $\mathcal J_i$. Neither the intervals nor the selected pair recover the
confidential source values. Table sensitivity, both suppression states, the
joint selection rule, and any product-specific exception must be retained in
provenance.

These equations apply only to a published OD row. A pair omitted under the
source product's below-six row-removal rule, or because workplace address is not
available at SA2, has no released cell and is not assigned $[0,5]$ or $[0,2]$.
Its mass remains unresolved source coverage unless a separate compatible
publisher total supplies a defensible bound.
