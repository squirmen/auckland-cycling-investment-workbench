Let $a_{ih}\in[0,1]$ be the share of OD relation $i$ assigned to declared
area-context stratum $h\in\mathcal{G}$, with $\sum_h a_{ih}=1$, and let
$\Delta y_{ic}\ge0$ be candidate-attributable outcome for candidate $c$.
Stratum totals and outcome shares are
\begin{align}
Y_{ch}
 &=\sum_{i\in\ODset}a_{ih}\Delta y_{ic},
\label{eq:equity-stratum-total}\\
s_{ch}^{Y}
 &=\frac{Y_{ch}}{\sum_{g\in\mathcal{G}}Y_{cg}}.
\label{eq:equity-outcome-share}
\end{align}
For comparison with a declared reference population or opportunity share
$s_h^{R}>0$, report the descriptive representation ratio
\begin{equation}
\rho_{ch}=\frac{s_{ch}^{Y}}{s_h^{R}}.
\label{eq:equity-representation-ratio}
\end{equation}
The reference denominator, missing geography, and unassigned outcome are
reported. These are area-level distributional summaries: they do not assign an
identity to a traveller, measure procedural justice, or prove who receives a
project's benefits or burdens.
