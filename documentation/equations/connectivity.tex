Let $D_i^{\mathrm{all}}$ be the shortest feasible physical route length and
$D_i^{\mathrm{LS}}$ the shortest route length using directed edges with traffic
stress at or below $\tau$. The pair-level pass indicator is
\begin{equation}
I_i^{\mathrm{LS}}
=I\!\left(D_i^{\mathrm{LS}}<\infty
\ \land\
D_i^{\mathrm{LS}}\le\delta D_i^{\mathrm{all}}\right).
\label{eq:connectivity-pass}
\end{equation}
For declared OD analysis set $\PriorityOD$ and non-negative demand weights $w_i$,
\begin{align}
\CIWLS
 &=\frac{1}{|\PriorityOD|}
   \sum_{i\in\PriorityOD}I_i^{\mathrm{LS}},
\label{eq:ciw-ls-unweighted}\\
\CIWLS_w
 &=\frac{\sum_{i\in\PriorityOD}w_iI_i^{\mathrm{LS}}}
          {\sum_{i\in\PriorityOD}w_i}.
\label{eq:ciw-ls-weighted}
\end{align}
These are the unweighted and demand-weighted CIW OD low-stress connectivity
shares for the declared set. They are not a universal city index.
For candidate metric $x_{cj}$, its min--max normalised value and score under
named preset $h$ are
\begin{align}
z_{cj}
 &=\begin{cases}
\dfrac{x_{cj}-\min_{c'}x_{c'j}}
      {\max_{c'}x_{c'j}-\min_{c'}x_{c'j}},
& \max_{c'}x_{c'j}>\min_{c'}x_{c'j},\\
\tfrac12, & \text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
\label{eq:preset-normalisation}\\
S_c^{(h)}
 &=\frac{\sum_j a_j^{(h)}z_{cj}}{\sum_j a_j^{(h)}},
\qquad a_j^{(h)}\ge0.
\label{eq:preset-score}
\end{align}
With declared maximised objectives $J_+$ and minimised objectives $J_-$, the
Pareto set is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{F}
=\left\{c\in\Candidates:
\nexists c'\ne c\ \text{that is no worse on every }j\in J_+\cup J_-
\text{ and strictly better on at least one}\right\}.
\label{eq:pareto-frontier}
\end{equation}
For an explicitly chosen ordered portfolio $B_r=(C_1,\ldots,C_r)$, each
treatment is applied to the preceding network and all routes are recomputed:
\begin{equation}
G_r=\mathcal{T}(G_{r-1},C_r),
\qquad
\Delta\mathbf y_r=\mathbf M(G_r)-\mathbf M(G_{r-1}).
\label{eq:portfolio-change}
\end{equation}
Preset scores and Pareto membership support comparison; neither selects a
globally optimal portfolio.
