For trip purpose $h\in\mathcal{H}$, let $F_{ab}^{(h)}$ be the source flow from
origin zone $a$ to destination zone $b$. Let $w_{az}^{O,h}$ and
$w_{bk}^{D,h}$ be non-negative within-zone origin and destination weights that
each sum to one. Disaggregated demand is
\begin{equation}
Q_{zk}^{(h)}
=\sum_a\sum_b F_{ab}^{(h)}w_{az}^{O,h}w_{bk}^{D,h},
\qquad
\sum_z\sum_k Q_{zk}^{(h)}=\sum_a\sum_b F_{ab}^{(h)}.
\label{eq:purpose-disaggregation}
\end{equation}
Population weights are used for residential origins; destination weights are
purpose-specific, such as employment, school enrolment, everyday opportunity,
or transit-boardings evidence. Terms with $a=b$ are retained, so intrazonal
flows are not discarded.

For retained plausible paths $r\in\mathcal{P}_{i}^{K,h}$ with conditional
probability $\pi_{ir}^{(h)}$, the directed edge flow is
\begin{equation}
X_e^{(h)}
=\sum_i Q_i^{(h)}
  \sum_{r\in\mathcal{P}_{i}^{K,h}}
  \pi_{ir}^{(h)}I(e\in P_{ir}),
\qquad
\sum_r\pi_{ir}^{(h)}=1.
\label{eq:purpose-edge-flow}
\end{equation}
Commute, school, everyday, and transit surfaces use distinct source flows,
weights, route ledgers, and provenance; a commute surface is not renamed to
stand in for another purpose.
